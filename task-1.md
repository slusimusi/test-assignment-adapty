## Introduction

Adapty Predictions help you answer the following questions:

- What is the predicted lifetime value (LTV) of your user cohorts?
- Which cohorts are likely to generate the highest revenue in the future?
- How much can you invest, being aware of the predicted payoff?

With Adapty Predictions, you can gain valuable insights and make data-driven decisions to optimize your revenue and growth strategy.

Adapty's prediction model is a powerful new feature. It uses machine learning for understanding your app's users' long-term revenue potential and behavior. With advanced gradient boosting techniques, the LTV prediction model can estimate the expected total revenue within the lifetime. This feature helps you to make informed decisions about user acquisition, marketing strategies, and product development.
Currently, the LTV prediction model provides two types of predictions: 
- predicted LTV 
- predicted revenue.

## Predictions in cohorts

Adapty can predict the LTV of users and their revenue for subscription-based apps. These predictions are available for 3, 6, and 12 months on the [cohort analysis page](https://adapty.io/docs/analytics-cohorts).

> [!WARNING]
> Currently, the model does not work for lifetimes and one-time purchases. 

Additionally, the accuracy of the LTV model may be lower for very new apps with limited data and for apps with significant changes in their user traffic.

### What is the prediction model?

The prediction model uses gradient boosting. Its highly accurate and efficient machine-learning algorithm handles large datasets. Our model can leverage transaction data from your subscription-based apps and predict the user's LTV a year after the creation of their profiles. The model uses the completely anonymized data. 

The model gathers data from all of your apps within Adapty for its training. However, Adapty further adjusts the predicted values to reflect the behavior patterns observed in each app’s cohorts. This post-correction algorithm takes into account the unique characteristics of each app for more accurate predictions.

The model achieves the highest accuracy level with a Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) of slightly below 10%. With this level of precision, businesses confidently rely on the model's predictions to make data-driven decisions.

### Types of the prediction model

Adapty uses two distinct gradient-boosting models to forecast LTV:

- Revenue prediction model: Predicts the total revenue generated by a cohort of users.
- LTV prediction model: Predicts the average LTV of users in a cohort.

Then, the trained models predict the total revenue and average LTV of each cohort within the selected period (3, 6, 9, or 12 months) after cohort creation. Adapty verifies and constrains these predictions to ensure their consistency with typical user behavior patterns.

The models initially generate predictions after 1-3 weeks following the creation of the cohort. This timeframe allows for sufficient data gathering and analysis. 

> [!NOTE]
> For example, we created a cohort on January 1st. Predictions for that cohort would be available sometime between January 15th and January 29th.

After the initial prediction generation, Adapty updates the predictions daily with the latest transactional data available for the cohort. In this case, the predictions remain current and reflect the most recent behavior of the cohort.

The model takes into account a variety of significant statistics about the cohorts, such as:
- revenue generated from past subscription periods
- user retention rates
- present LTV
- subscription type
- the proportion of users from Google Play and App Store
- geographic distribution of users by country, among others. 

These features guarantee the model captures the most pertinent information for precise forecasting about the future user LTV.

The model typically reflects changes in product performance, such as increased retention for monthly subscriptions, with a delay of approximately one week.

### Limitations of the prediction model

The prediction model has certain limitations. You should take them into account while interpreting its results. These limitations include:

- Data quality: The model's performance depends on the quality and representativeness of the available data. Data quality is a crucial aspect of data analysis. One of the main reasons for insufficient results is unusual cohort behavior. In this case, it deviates from normal cohort metrics for a particular app or all other ones. This type of data is uncommon for the model and can lead to unexpected results. This situation is more common for new, unusual products or new apps that haven't been included in the training set.
- Time frame: The model can predict values up to 12 months from the creation of a user cohort. In this case, you see growing actual revenue. However, the prediction remains at the point of 12 months.
- Subscription durations: The model shows the best performance on monthly and weekly subscriptions

## Prediction in Adapty cohorts

To access predicted revenue and LTV for your subscribers, navigate to the Cohort Analyses page in your Adapty dashboard. Learn more about the [Adapty cohort](https://adapty.io/docs/analytics-cohorts).

### Types of prediction in Adapty cohorts

The predicted revenue (pRevenue) column shows the estimated total revenue a cohort of subscribers is expected to generate during the selected time frame after cohort creation. To predict revenue for users, Adapty's revenue prediction model calculates this value with advanced gradient boosting techniques.

The predicted LTV (pLTV) column shows the estimated lifetime value of each user in the selected cohort. The formula is `the estimated LTV = the predicted revenue / the predicted number of paying users in the cohort`. To predict the number of paying users in a cohort, Adapty's base prediction model calculates the predicted number of paying users. 

To define the time period for displaying the predicted revenue and predicted LTV, select the desired value from the timeframe dropdown in the user interface. The available options are typically 3, 6, 9, or 12 months after cohort creation.

To provide even more valuable insights, you can filter predicted revenue and LTV by product. By default, Adapty builds predictions with all purchase data. However, with filtering by product, you better understand how each product performs and contributes to your overall predicted revenue and LTV.

### Limitations of prediction in Adapty cohorts

While using predictions in Adapty cohorts, you can encounter some limitation issues.

The first limitation issue is insufficient data available for accurate predictions. 
The user interface greys out the corresponding fields. You can hover over the fields and notice a tooltip with the message "Insufficient data for accurate prediction". This serves as a visual cue that the predicted values may not be reliable. Further data collection and analysis may be necessary to generate accurate predictions. The lack of data may occur due to several reasons, such as insufficient time since cohort creation, a small cohort size, or an unpopular subscription type. As mentioned before, the cohort may misbehave and deviate from the normal metrics. Waiting a few weeks may help resolve this issue.

Another limitation issue is the lack of values for the prediction. 
Empty results may occur if there is either insufficient data to make any predictions (usually at least 1-3 weeks of data is needed) or if the maximum time frame for prediction has already passed, i.e., it has been more than a year since the cohort was created.

> [!WARNING]
> When upgrading your pricing plan to the Pro+ or Enterprise, the prediction data for Revenue and LTV may become available on your Adapty dashboard within 24 hours.
